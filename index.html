FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about working with Aarush Designer.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most projects take 2–4 weeks from discovery to launch, depending on complexity. A simple landing page can be ready in 7–10 days.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes! I offer monthly website care plans that include updates, backups, security monitoring and SEO improvements.

What's included in a typical project?

Every project includes premium design, responsive development, SEO setup, and 30 days of technical support. E-commerce and custom features are available.

Do you offer a money-back guarantee?

Yes — your satisfaction is guaranteed. If any personal data is leaked due to our negligence, we offer a full refund according to our privacy policy.

Can you help with SEO and ranking?

Absolutely. Every site I build includes technical SEO, Google Search Console setup, and on-page optimization to help you rank from day one.

What is the cost of a website?

Pricing starts from ₹7,999 for a single landing page and goes up based on features. Book a consultation for a custom quote.
