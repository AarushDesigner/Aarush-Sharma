Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Overview

We want every client to be confident in what they're paying for. This policy explains how refunds are handled for project work and monthly management plans.

2. Advance Payments

Advance payments confirm your project slot and cover initial discovery, planning and design work. Once design work has begun, the advance is non-refundable, as time and resources have already been committed to your project.

3. Milestone Payments

For projects billed in milestones, payments already made for completed stages (e.g. approved wireframes, approved UI design) are non-refundable. If a project is cancelled mid-way, you will only be billed for work completed up to that point.

4. Cancellation Before Work Begins

If a project is cancelled before any design or development work has started, a partial refund of the advance may be issued at our discretion, minus any consultation or planning time already spent.

5. Monthly Website Management Plan

The ₹5,000/month management plan is billed in advance for that month's services. Since work (backups, monitoring, updates) begins immediately each billing cycle, monthly fees are non-refundable once the cycle has started. You may cancel anytime to stop future billing.

6. Third-Party Costs

Costs paid on your behalf to third parties (domain registration, hosting, premium plugins, stock assets) are non-refundable once purchased, as these are governed by the third-party provider's own policies.

7. Quality Issues

If delivered work does not match the agreed scope, we will revise it at no additional cost within the agreed revision rounds. Refunds for quality concerns are considered only if issues cannot be resolved through revision.

8. How to Request a Refund

Email sarush066@gmail.com with your project details and reason for the request. Refund requests are reviewed within 5–7 business days.

9. Contact Us
